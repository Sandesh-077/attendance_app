# CA Attendance
## Teacher Quick Start Guide — Version 1.1

### 1. Installation
1. Download or open **CA-Attendance-v1.1.0.apk** from the school's official source.
2. If Android asks, allow installation from the trusted app you used to open the file.
3. Tap **Install**, then open **CA Attendance**. You can turn that installation permission off again afterward.
4. Keep the app installed for future updates; avoid unnecessary uninstalling and reinstalling.

### 2. Sign up and login
If you do not have an account, tap **Sign Up** on the login screen and enter your name, email address, and password. New accounts automatically receive the teacher (`user`) role. Ask the school administrator to assign your classes; they will not appear until assigned. If the school has already provided an account, sign in with that account. Do not share your login details. If you cannot remember your password, tap **Forgot Password?** on the login screen and follow the reset instructions.

### 3. Teacher Dashboard
After login, the welcome screen links to **My Classes**, **Attendance**, and **Profile**. **My Classes** shows classes assigned to you. Contact an administrator if an expected class is missing.

### 4. Class Roster
Open **My Classes**, choose a class, then tap **Students**. The roster shows active students in that class. Tap the search icon and enter part of a student's name to find them.

### 5. Taking Attendance
Open **Attendance** or an assigned class, choose the class, and tap **Take Attendance**. Check the date, then mark each student **Present**, **Absent**, or **Late**. Tap **Review and save attendance** and confirm. Use **Report** beside a student to record an issue; it is separate from their attendance status.

If you press Back or change the date before saving your marks, the app asks whether to continue or leave and discard those changes.

### 6. Reporting Student Issues
Tap **Report** for the student, choose a reason and severity (**Minor**, **Moderate**, or **Serious**), add details when requested, then tap **Submit report**. Some reasons require details; serious reports require an explanation. Contact an administrator if a report needs follow-up.

### 7. Attendance History
From a class, open **Attendance History** to review records by date. Student views may also show an individual attendance history. If a record looks wrong, raise it with the administrator.

### 8. Updating CA Attendance
The app may show a notification when a new version is available. Tap **Update** and follow the school's official download link. **Later** may be available for optional updates. Install APKs only from the school's official link.
